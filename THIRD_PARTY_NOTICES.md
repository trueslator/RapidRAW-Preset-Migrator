# Third-party notices

RapidRAW Preset Migrator does not bundle third-party runtime libraries.

## RapidRAW

RapidRAW is a separate open-source project by CyberTimon and contributors and is licensed under AGPL-3.0. This migrator does not include RapidRAW source code or binaries; it reads/writes the preset interchange/storage structures used by an installed copy of RapidRAW.

## Adobe Lightroom / DNG Camera Profiles

Adobe Lightroom and the DNG/DCP ecosystem are separate third-party technologies. This project does not include Adobe software or profile assets. Users provide their own legacy `.lrtemplate` and optional `.dcp` files.

## User-provided presets and profiles

Commercial and community preset/profile packs may have independent licenses. Those licenses continue to apply to the source assets and may restrict distribution of converted outputs.
